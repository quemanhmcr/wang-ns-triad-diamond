# Causal binary ancestry versus sticky reuse

Fresh critical energy by itself does **not** rule out a single infinite geometric cascade: the energy cost `~1/N_q` is summable.  The correct extra structure is quadratic causality.  A genuinely new comparable-frequency companion at a later generation must itself have parents in the preceding causal layer, unless it is imported from an already existing reservoir.  Backwards in time, fresh ancestry therefore branches binary; reuse merges those branches.

This note records the exact finite layered theorem.  It is conditional on a synchronized causal packet layering and is not yet a theorem that every Navier--Stokes Duhamel expansion admits such a layering with summable interfaces.

## Signed-good scale synchronization

On the signed-good extremal core,

\[
x=r_*e^{-v-u/2},\qquad y=r_*e^{-v+u/2},
\]

with `0<=u<=1/200`, `|v|<=1/100`.  The old lower estimate was `>3/5`.  Arb also gives the matching clean upper estimate

\[
\boxed{
\frac35<\frac{N_{parent}}{N_{child}}<\frac58.
}
\]

Thus every forward causal edge advances scale by

\[
\boxed{
\frac85<\frac{N_{child}}{N_{parent}}<\frac53.
}
\]

## Layered incidence identities

Let levels be `j=0,...,L`, with one terminal packet `n_L=1`.  Every packet at level `j+1` is generated by one active triad with two parent incidences at level `j`, and level `j` contains exactly the distinct parents used by the next level.  Let `n_j` be the number of distinct packet nodes.

Define the parent-reuse savings

\[
\boxed{r_j=2n_{j+1}-n_j\ge0}
\]

and the reuse fraction

\[
\boxed{
ho_j={r_j\over2n_{j+1}}.}
\]

For the connected 3-uniform packet/triad incidence graph, with `m=sum_{j=1}^L n_j` triad vertices and `n=sum_{j=0}^L n_j` packet vertices,

\[
\beta=3m-(n+m)+1=2m-n+1.
\]

A direct telescope gives

\[
\boxed{
\beta=\sum_{j=0}^{L-1}r_j.
}
\]

So cycle rank is exactly the total number of binary parent slots saved by reuse.

The multiplicative form is even more useful:

\[
\boxed{
{n_0\over2^L}
=\prod_{j=0}^{L-1}{n_j\over2n_{j+1}}
=\prod_{j=0}^{L-1}(1-\rho_j).
}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal A_{reuse}
:=\sum_j-\log(1-\rho_j)
=L\log2-\log n_0.
}
\]

A full binary tree has `rho_j=0`, `beta=0`, `n_0=2^L`.  Every merge/reuse lowers the number of distinct root ancestors and creates cycle rank.

## Finite coherent energy forces reuse action

Let `N_base` be the distinguished lineage scale at level zero.  The forward upper progress `N_child/N_parent<5/3`, followed by the backward parent ratio `<5/8`, gives for any root ancestor of a depth-`L` terminal packet

\[
\boxed{
N_{root}<\left(\frac{25}{24}\right)^L N_{base}.
}
\]

Suppose distinct root coherent cells have positive energy budget

\[
\sum_{root}E_a\le P E_{global}
\]

and each selected affine grain has scale-critical mass

\[
N_aE_a\ge\eta.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
n_0\le{P E_{global}N_{base}\over\eta}
\left(\frac{25}{24}\right)^L.
}
\]

Combining with the binary product law,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal A_{reuse}
\ge
L\log\frac{48}{25}
-\log\frac{P E_{global}N_{base}}{\eta}.
}
\]

For Moyal cell energy `P=1`, and the clean shell/affine-grain mass `eta=1/5`, this is

\[
\mathcal A_{reuse}
\ge
L\log\frac{48}{25}-\log(5E_{global}N_{base}).
\]

If every layer had `rho_j<1/4`, then

\[
\mathcal A_{reuse}<L\log\frac43.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
L\log\frac{36}{25}>\log(5E_{global}N_{base})
\Longrightarrow
\max_j\rho_j\ge\frac14.
}
\]

A sufficiently deep causally complete ancestry must therefore contain a **reuse-rich layer**: at least one quarter of its formal binary parent slots are identified with already used parents.  The layered cycle identity says those identifications are genuine cycle-rank units, not a bookkeeping artifact.

This is the causal/sticky mechanism that energy alone was missing: the number of possible fresh causal ancestors grows like two per generation, while the signed-good physical scale can dilate only by at most `5/3`; finite coherent energy therefore forces branch merging/reuse.

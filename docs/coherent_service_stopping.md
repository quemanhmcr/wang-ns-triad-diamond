# Coherent service stopping: finite lifetime of a cost-free old-reservoir epoch

The coherent increment theorem gives a blockwise alternative, while whole-old-pool erosion gives geometric decay across generations.  Combining them produces a stopping theorem.

Let

\[
r=\frac{231525}{512000}<\frac12
\]

be the certified physical-energy service ratio of a materially reused low-frequency pool on a signed-good low-strain lineage.  Thus

\[
C_{old}(q)\le C_0r^q.
\]

Suppose every efficient block in one ancestry epoch has coherent square-service threshold

\[
Y_q\ge Y_0>0.
\]

If `d_high<Y0/4`, the actual low-band coherent service is at least `Y0/2`.  If in addition

\[
C_{old}(q)\le Y_0/8,
\]

the coherent service-edge theorem says the old pool cannot carry the block.  One of the following must occur:

\[
\boxed{d_{high}\ge Y_0/4,}
\]

\[
\boxed{\Xi_{cell}\ge Y_0/8,}
\]

\[
\boxed{\mu_{coh,new}\ge Y_0/32,}
\]

\[
\boxed{H_{ancestry}\ge\log2,}
\]

or

\[
\boxed{Q_{ancestry}-Q_{atomic}\ge1/4.}
\]

Therefore a cost-free old-pool epoch has length strictly below the first `q_*` with

\[
\boxed{C_0r^{q_*}\le Y_0/8.}
\]

A relinking may restart the geometric clock, but the relinking itself is already a charged interface/fresh/cycle event.  This is the first direct stopping-time bridge from the filtered Navier--Stokes coherent-service currency to the multiscale master architecture.

The remaining continuum problem is no longer reservoir persistence.  It is to prove a **uniform lower threshold `Y0`** on every efficient PDE block (or route the block into the already existing flat/Hodge/source alternatives) and then telescope the charged epochs with the transfer-weighted `Xi` budget.

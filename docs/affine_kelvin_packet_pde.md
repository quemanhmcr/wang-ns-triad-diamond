# Exact affine low--high packet PDE: Navier--Stokes -> Kelvin -> objective spinor

The strict low-pass separation in `smooth_sgs_affine_packet_equation.md` shows
that a selected near-extremal role cannot be created by `V\otimes V` when
`supp Vhat` lies below `N/4`.  The self low--high interaction is therefore the
physical transporter of that role.  This note shows that its affine jet gives
**exactly** the Kelvin/helical dynamics used elsewhere in the repository.

## 1. Fourier equation of the affine linearization

Work in coordinates moving with the packet center and let

\[
V(x)=Ax,\qquad \operatorname{tr}A=0.
\]

A divergence-free perturbation `w` satisfies the affine linearized equation

\[
\partial_tw+(Ax\cdot\nabla)w+Aw+\nabla\pi=\nu\Delta w,
\qquad \nabla\cdot w=0.
\]

Fourier transformation gives

\[
\partial_t\widehat w-(A^T\xi)\cdot\nabla_\xi\widehat w
+A\widehat w+i\xi\widehat\pi
=-\nu|\xi|^2\widehat w.
\]

The pressure is not an independent source.  Differentiating
`xi dot what=0` along the Fourier characteristic determines it exactly:

\[
i\widehat\pi
=-2\frac{\xi\cdot A\widehat w}{|\xi|^2}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\left(\partial_t-(A^T\xi)\cdot\nabla_\xi\right)\widehat w
=G_A(\xi)\widehat w-\nu|\xi|^2\widehat w,
}
\]

with

\[
\boxed{
G_A(\xi)=-A+2\widehat\xi\widehat\xi^TA.
}
\]

Along characteristics this is precisely

\[
\boxed{
\dot k=-A^Tk,
\qquad
\dot a=\left[-A+2\widehat k\widehat k^TA\right]a-\nu|k|^2a.
}
\]

This is the Kelvin/Craik--Criminale mechanism arising directly from the
low--high part of Navier--Stokes.  Exact Navier--Stokes shear-wave solutions in
the Craik--Criminale/Kelvin class provide the physical precedent for this
transport law; the repository uses it locally as the osculating low-frequency
jet, not as a turbulence closure.

## 2. Moving cell and transverse frame

For a scalar Fourier cell `m(t,xi)`, define

\[
D_A=\partial_t-(A^T\xi)\cdot\nabla_\xi.
\]

Then

\[
D_A(m\widehat w)=(D_Am)\widehat w+mD_A\widehat w.
\]

Hence a cell satisfying

\[
\boxed{D_Am=0}
\]

moves with the Kelvin dual flow and introduces **zero** spectral-cell leakage.
This is the strong PDE meaning of the Heisenberg commutator certificate in the
smooth-SGS packet module.

Let `n=k/|k|` and let `E=(e_1,e_2)` be an orthonormal frame of `n^perp`.  The
normal direction obeys

\[
\dot n=-(I-nn^T)A^Tn.
\]

Choose the free frame spin objectively by

\[
E^T\dot E=-\operatorname{skew}(E^TAE).
\]

If `a=Ec`, direct differentiation of the 3D Kelvin amplitude gives

\[
\boxed{
\dot c
=-\operatorname{sym}(E^TAE)c-\nu|k|^2c.
}
\]

Thus the exact low--high PDE, the Kelvin carrier law, and the objective 2x2
polarization generator are the **same transport equation in three coordinate
systems**.

Rigid transverse rotation is gauge; symmetric strain is the physical
polarization generator.  The common-parent `SL(2)` quotient and relative
polarization theorem then determine which part of that generator the nonlinear
triad can actually detect.

## 3. Consequence for the actual packet residual

After this exact affine low--high motion is removed, the remaining self-transport
forcing is generated by `V-V_aff`.  Its quadratic Taylor jet is already split by
the affine/Hermite modules into

- scalar `H_3` envelope sideband from `Sym B`;
- vector `H_1` polarization sideband from spatial strain variation;
- common quadratic phase/chirp, which is tangent/gauge.

Thus **common low--high transport creates no direct degree-zero spinor forcing at
first order**.  Degree-zero forcing is left to role-dependent/cross interactions
and higher-order sideband backreaction.  The former is identified at work level
with the existing trilinear cross-error `Xi`; the latter remains a genuine
spacetime coherence problem.

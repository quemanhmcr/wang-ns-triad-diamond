# Strain coherence and the objective velocity-gradient branch

The affine-grain theorem charges a frozen trace-free planar strain.  A genuine
Navier--Stokes packet sees a time-dependent velocity gradient, so the next
question is whether fast strain variation can evade that cost.  This note gives
a quantitative answer in the co-rotating planar model and identifies the exact
PDE source of any failure of coherence.

## 1. Co-rotating strain is the physical variable

Write

\[
A=\nabla u=S+\Omega,
\qquad S^T=S,\quad \Omega^T=-\Omega.
\]

Kelvin carriers obey

\[
\dot k=(-S+\Omega)k.
\]

Choose the frame `R_dot=Omega R`.  In that frame the rigid local rotation is
removed and carrier shape is driven by `R^T S R`.  Thus temporal rotation of the
laboratory axes must not itself be mistaken for multiplier deformation.

Let `D(t)` denote the trace-free symmetric restriction to an invariant extremal
triad plane in this co-rotating frame.  Suppose `D(0)` has eigenvalues `+/-d`.

## 2. Five-percent coherence still forces a definite cost

Assume

\[
\sup_{0\le t\le T}\|D(t)-D(0)\|_{op}\le\frac d{20},
\qquad
 dT\le\frac1{30}.
\]

For a unit carrier direction `n`, its logarithmic length rate is
`alpha=-n^T D n`.  Since `||D(t)||<=21d/20`, the direction speed is at most
`2(21/20)d`.  Relative to the initial rate this gives

\[
|\alpha(t)-\alpha(0)|
\le \frac d{20}+4\frac{21}{20}d^2t.
\]

Passing to the Hodge vector `z=(u/sqrt2,sqrt2 v)` amplifies a common log-rate
error by at most `sqrt(10)`.  Combining this with the certified initial speed
`|z_dot(0)|^2>0.86d^2` yields

\[
|z(t)|\ge dt\left[
\sqrt{0.86}-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{20}
-2\sqrt{10}\frac{21}{20}dt
\right].
\]

At `dt<=1/30`, Arb proves the squared bracket is `>1/4`.  Also

\[
|u|,|v|\le2\frac{21}{20}dT=\frac7{100}<\frac2{25},
\]

so the whole episode remains inside the certified local single-edge box.  Hence

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal H(t)=\frac{u^2}{2}+2v^2\ge\frac14d^2t^2
}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\frac1T\int_0^T\operatorname{Def}(t)dt
\ge\frac1{24}(dT)^2.
}
\]

Therefore a low-cost packet with significant non-conformal strain cannot evade
the affine-grain cost merely by a small time dependence.

## 3. Failure of coherence is itself a quantitative event

If at some time

\[
\|D(t)-D(0)\|_{op}>\frac d{20},
\]

then total variation forces

\[
\boxed{
\int_0^T\|\dot D(t)\|_{op}dt>\frac d{20}.
}
\]

Thus the alternative to multiplier cost is a definite **objective strain
variation**.  The key physical point is that Navier--Stokes gives an exact law
for this variation.

## 4. Navier--Stokes tells us what changes the strain

For a smooth incompressible solution, along a material trajectory,

\[
\boxed{
D_tA+A^2=-\nabla^2p+\nu\Delta A.
}
\]

Taking the symmetric part gives

\[
D_tS=-S^2-\Omega^2-\nabla^2p+\nu\Delta S.
\]

In the frame rotating with `Omega`, the objective/corotational strain derivative
is

\[
\boxed{
\mathring S
:=D_tS+S\Omega-\Omega S
=-S^2-\Omega^2-\nabla^2p+\nu\Delta S
+S\Omega-\Omega S.
}
\]

Consequently the "non-frozen strain" branch is not a free compactness error.  It
must be generated by a combination of self-stretching, vorticity rotation,
pressure Hessian, and viscous smoothing.  This is the correct next currency for
the PDE ledger.

## 5. Relation to the packet lifetime

For `T=cN^{-2}`, the coherent branch pays

\[
\frac{c^2}{24}\left(\frac d{N^2}\right)^2.
\]

If this cost is absent while `d/N^2` is non-negligible, the objective strain must
change by a fixed percentage within one parabolic lifetime.  A future closure
can therefore target the scale-critical action of `mathring S`, rather than
trying to control arbitrary time dependence of the packet profile.

The present theorem assumes an invariant planar reduction.  Off-plane strain
which tilts the triad plane, and the spatial variation of `A` across the packet,
remain explicit perturbation variables to be charged by the affine-window
curvature ledger.

# Mixed genuine-owner recurrence: certified barriers against false closure

Status: **DRAFT PROOF-GUARD CANDIDATE — independent PR, not certified, not merge-ready.**

After reducing the historical vocabulary to native event roots, several tempting one-line closures become visible. The existing certified theorems already forbid them. This note records those prohibitions as load-bearing guards for every later mixed-owner argument.

## 1. Critical mass is not a finite event budget

A shell-critical event carries a normalized quantity of the form

\[
N E_N\ge \mu_*>0.
\]

This does not imply a scale-independent amount of global energy is consumed. On a purely arithmetic geometric allocation

\[
N_j=N_0r^j,\qquad N_jE_j=\mu_*,\qquad r>1,
\]

we have

\[
E_j=\frac{\mu_*}{N_0}r^{-j},
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{j\ge0}E_j
=\frac{\mu_*}{N_0}\frac{r}{r-1}<\infty.
}
\]

This is not an existence example for a Navier--Stokes trajectory. It is enough to prove that the global `L2` energy budget **alone** cannot count critical events. The certified physical multi-currency master states the same principle for both critical `NE` and normalized `D_V`: neither becomes an additive reset without a separate scale-independent physical lower in a genuinely globally bounded resource.

## 2. Gross radial traffic is not a finite budget

The certified radial layer-cake theorem gives real low-to-high and high-to-low Fourier-radius crossing, but it also proves:

- high-to-high circulation cancels from the tail boundary law;
- equiradial physical energy transfer can be nonzero with exactly zero radial displacement;
- no minimum dyadic/logarithmic step follows;
- gross crossing or gross logarithmic action is not promoted to a finite traffic resource.

Hence a mixed-owner proof may use the **signed/native** radial identity where appropriate, but may not count event depth by total upward/downward traffic or by an invented minimum log-scale step.

## 3. Generic shell registration is not scale progress

For an incoming hard shell at frequency `M`, the generic service theorem sets

\[
A=3M/4
\]

only to register a smooth analysis carrier which equals one on the hard shell. Its own theorem explicitly says supplier-specific scale progress relative to the preceding block is a separate geometric statement and is **not fabricated** by the generic shell theorem.

The endpoint hard-shell witnesses of a full survivor can occur at `A` or `2A`, i.e. ratios `3/4` or `3/2` relative to `M`. Therefore `A=3M/4` cannot be multiplied through a mixed path as a monotone master scale factor.

This blocks, in particular, the seductive but invalid attempt to combine the certified consecutive high-strain `3/16` ratio with arbitrary source-shell registrations to manufacture a universal source--strain descent telescope.

## 4. Fresh source/service scale has an upper bound, not a direction

The certified refinement-invariant fresh-service theorem pushes actual positive fresh service to the fixed LP band law. For the relevant parent bands its two candidate hard shells satisfy at most

\[
M_{next}\le2N.
\]

The theorem explicitly refuses to infer signed-good progress or a direction relative to the previous block. Thus `M_next<=2N` is not a contraction and cannot be used as one.

Material freshness also cannot repair this: the native material factorization in this draft treats NN as provenance on an already-existing positive service law, not as new energy creation.

## 5. Consequence

The surviving three-root problem

\[
\text{source/service}
\;|\;
\text{strain/dissipation}
\;|\;
\text{actual HH/hard-tail work}
\]

cannot be closed by any scalar budget made from critical shell mass, normalized dissipation, gross radial traffic, generic carrier scale, or fresh-scale selection.

A valid mixed theorem must add **new native information**, for example a type-correct cross-owner transition identity or a physical ledger whose quantity is genuinely shared by the relevant roots. It may not invent an exchange rate among their existing heterogeneous currencies.

No Navier--Stokes global-regularity claim is made.

Using `dot K_NL=2 kappa(0)` and

\Delta_{rad}=EZ-K^2=E^2\sigma^2,

\boxed{

Thus the earlier product

`critical alignment * Re_spec * sqrt(defect fraction * decorrelation)`

was only a factorization of one normalized curvature height.

x=\sigma/m,

## 38. Sharp Galilean null law for the critical helicity-flip boost

The commutator form of `S_u` supplies the missing behavior at the low-radius edge without invoking a cutoff.  Consider one Fourier triangle

\[
q+\ell-k=0
\]

and a unit helical velocity mode at radius `q`.  Let the critical input at `ell` have helicity `s` and the output at `k` have helicity `-s`.  Same-sign input/output has zero matrix element in `S_u` because `S_u` anticommutes with `mathsf J`.

For the helicity-flip matrix element, cyclic invariance of the Waleffe triple product gives

\[
\boxed{
|\langle h_{k,-s},S_{u_q}h_{\ell,s}\rangle|
=2\sqrt{|k||\ell|}\,|g(q,\ell,-k)|\,|a_q|
}
\]

for one unit-normalized helical coefficient `a_q` of the advecting velocity.

Let

\[
m=\frac{|k|+|\ell|}{|q|}>1,
\qquad
x=\frac{|k|-|\ell|}{|q|},
\qquad |x|<1.
\]

The positive sum/difference form of Heron is

\[
A_\triangle
=\frac{|q|^2}{4}
\sqrt{(m^2-1)(1-x^2)}.
\]

Because the output/input helicities are opposite, maximizing over the helicity of the advecting mode gives the signed-frequency factor

\[
|q|+\big||k|-|\ell|\big|
=|q|(1+|x|).
\]

Therefore

\[
\frac{2\sqrt{|k||\ell|}|g|}{|q|}
=\frac{1+|x|}{2\sqrt2}
\sqrt{
\frac{(m^2-1)(1-x^2)}{m^2-x^2}
}.
\]

For fixed `x` the last square-root increases with `m` and tends to `sqrt(1-x^2)`.  The remaining one-variable maximization

\[
(1+x)\sqrt{1-x^2},
\qquad 0\le x<1,
\]

has its unique maximum at `x=1/2`.  Hence the sharp supremum is

\[
\boxed{
2\sqrt{|k||\ell|}|g(q,\ell,-k)|
\le
\frac{3\sqrt6}{16}|q|.
}
\]

The constant is approached by `m->infinity`, `|x|->1/2`; it is not obtained by a finite collinear triangle.  A `500,000`-triangle stress saw no violation, and direct comparison against the repository closed-form Waleffe coupling on another `8,000` random physical triangles passed the same bound.

This is an exact **Galilean derivative law**.  No matter how large the target curl radius becomes, one fixed large-scale mode can feed the critical helicity-changing boost only through its gradient scale `|q||a_q|`.  A uniform mode (`q=0`) is pure sweeping and commutes with the helicity involution exactly.

Thus the radial near-extremizer and the nonlinear driver pull in opposite directions:

- radial viscous impedance is cheapest when one participating radius is driven toward zero;
- the critical boost produced by that radius simultaneously loses strength linearly and becomes transport symmetry.

This does not by itself close the full many-mode problem: arbitrarily many strain-producing modes can cooperate.  But it removes the possibility of an indefinitely reusable **fixed low-frequency catalyst**.  Any escape which keeps the boost comparable to `nu N^2` must make the strain-producing spectrum itself climb or concentrate.  That is a continuous operator statement, not a new low/high owner branch.

## 39. Critical viscosity and the absolute-curl companion complete one exact Hilbert square

There is a second exact critical balance which does not pass through radial moments.  Let

\[
\Lambda=|C|,
\qquad
R_u=\mathbb P(u\times\Lambda u).

`R_u` is an intrinsic **dual/companion field**, not a new physical Euler force: it is obtained by inserting the gradient of the actual critical observable `K/2` in place of the helicity gradient in the same Lamb operator.  The physical critical source satisfies

\[
\begin{aligned}

&=\langle\Lambda u,u\times\omega\rangle\\
&=-\langle\omega,R_u\rangle.
\end{aligned}
\]

Put

\[
A=\Lambda^{1/2}\omega,
\qquad
B=\Lambda^{-1/2}R_u.
\]

Then

\[
\|A\|_2^2=M_3,
\qquad
\langle A,B\rangle=-\kappa(0).
\]

The full critical balance therefore has the exact completed-square form

\[
\boxed{
\dot K
=-2\nu\left\|A+\frac{B}{2\nu}\right\|_2^2
+\frac{1}{2\nu}\|B\|_2^2.

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{

=-2\nu\left\|
\Lambda^{1/2}\omega
+\frac{1}{2\nu}\Lambda^{-1/2}\mathbb P(u\times\Lambda u)

This is an identity, not Young's inequality inserted after the fact.  The positive upper remainder is scale-critical in spacetime.  In particular

\[

but the converse is false because the Hilbert angle matters.  On the direct Galerkin referee,

while `K' ~= -0.0291`; the square identity still matched the direct PDE rate to `1.1e-15`.  Thus `||B||/(nu||A||)` is only a necessary activity threshold, never a causal or sufficient criterion.

The same identity has a two-point interpretation.  For the positive periodic kernel of `Lambda`, let `D omega(x,y)=omega(x)-omega(y)` and `A_u(x,y)=u(x) x u(y)`.  The fractional Dirichlet form gives

Only the exact/coboundary component of the antisymmetric pair-area field can pair with `D omega`; its cycle component is critical-work-invisible.  The triangle circulation

\frac{1}{q}(M_\phi)'_{\triangle,NL}
=p_-\phi(a)+p_+\phi(b)-\phi(m)

This is the deterministic three-point Itô/Tanaka grammar already hidden in the divided-difference theorem:

- affine `phi` has zero curvature, giving energy/helicity conservation;
- `phi=a^2` reads quadratic variation, giving vortex stretching;
- `phi=|a|` reads the kink at the helicity interface `a=0`, giving critical `H^{1/2}` pair creation; on one-sign support it is affine and the source vanishes;

At continuum level the positive tent in the curl-curvature theorem is exactly the potential of this elementary martingale increment.  The signed curvature current `kappa` is a superposition of spread tents and reverse-spread tents.  Viscosity contributes the separate native killing

The only part not encoded by the one-dimensional curl state is the vertical shape motion which determines how coherently many physical triads can drive the next spread.

### Continuous no-free-progress corollary

The martingale geometry also removes the need for a fixed signed-good progress threshold on a strict heterochiral UV-frontier spread.  Normalize the high recipient radius to one, let `D` be the same-helicity median donor radius and `S` the opposite-helicity side-recipient radius.  Then

and the child upper-parent log-progress work is

F_c=W_c\log\frac1{\max(D,S)}.

\boxed{F_c\le2W_s.}

## 16. Isolated triad phase is a rotation of one fixed nonlinear speed

The Fisher law can be lifted from modal-energy rates back to the three complex helical amplitudes.  In the common cyclic Waleffe gauge an isolated three-wave Euler system has

\[
\boxed{
\dot A_0=2(a_1-a_2)g\,\overline{A_1A_2},
}
\]

with the two cyclic companions.  Let `e_i=|A_i|^2`.  A direct sum gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_i|\dot A_i|^2
&=4|g|^2\big[(a_1-a_2)^2e_1e_2
 +(a_2-a_0)^2e_2e_0
 +(a_0-a_1)^2e_0e_1\big]\\
&=\boxed{4|g|^2\Delta}.
\end{aligned}
\]

So for fixed instantaneous modal energies and geometry the **total nonlinear amplitude speed is independent of the common triad phase**.

For each complex modal line, decompose `dot A_i` into the real radial component parallel to `A_i` and the quadrature component parallel to `iA_i`.  If

\[
P=A_0A_1A_2\bar g
=|g|\sqrt{e_0e_1e_2}\,e^{i\Phi},
\]

then exactly

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal A_{rad}=4|g|^2\Delta\cos^2\Phi,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{phase}=4|g|^2\Delta\sin^2\Phi,
}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal A_{rad}+\mathcal A_{phase}=4|g|^2\Delta.
}
\]

The first term is precisely the Fisher--Rao spectral energy speed of Section 14.  The second changes complex phase/shape while carrying no instantaneous modal-energy work.  Therefore phase cancellation does not switch the nonlinearity off: it rotates the same physical nonlinear velocity from energy redistribution into state reorientation.

There is also an exact isolated-triad phase invariant.  Differentiate the same cubic product:

\[
\dot P
=2|g|^2\big[
(a_1-a_2)e_1e_2
+(a_2-a_0)e_2e_0
+(a_0-a_1)e_0e_1
\big]\in\mathbb R.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Im}(A_0A_1A_2\bar g)=\text{constant}}
\]

for the isolated Euler triad.

Together with energy and helicity, this reduces the three-wave energy dynamics to one real coordinate.  Indeed

\[
(\operatorname{Re}P)^2
=|g|^2e_0e_1e_2-(\operatorname{Im}P)^2,
\]

while the two affine invariants make two of the `e_i` affine functions of the third.  The isolated transfer therefore moves in a compact one-dimensional cubic potential and reaches turning points where `Re P=0`; it cannot supply a one-way unbounded curl escape by itself.

This does **not** give a global triad-by-triad invariant in Navier--Stokes.  In the full field, one Fourier/helical amplitude belongs to many simultaneous triads.  Their vertical shape velocities change each other's phases and destroy the isolated cubic invariant.  The important reduction is conceptual:

\[
\boxed{
\text{global cascade requires cooperative triad interference to continually reorient otherwise self-contained three-wave currents.}
}
\]

The remaining primitive problem is therefore not a list of owner types.  It is whether this cooperative reorientation can stay coherent strongly enough, for long enough, against the same quadratic curl killing, to drive an infinite pre-singular martingale-spread chain.

## 17. Critical growth is radial variance times sign--radius decorrelation, not a crude helicity product

The nonnegative split `Delta=Delta_rad+Delta_hel` identifies two boundary faces, but it does **not** imply a product bound on the critical source.  Direct adversarial spectral states disprove the tempting estimate `E D_|a| <= Delta_rad Delta_hel` by large factors.  The exact variable is a covariance.

Define

\[
L:=\int a|a|\,d\rho(a)
\]

and the centered signed-radius covariance numerator

\[
C_{a,|a|}:=EL-HK.
\]

Then direct Gram algebra gives

\[
\boxed{
E\,\mathcal D_{|a|}
=\Delta\,\Delta_{rad}-C_{a,|a|}^2.
}
\]

When `Delta Delta_rad>0`, put

\[
\varrho
:=\frac{EL-HK}{\sqrt{\Delta\Delta_{rad}}}.
\]

This is exactly the correlation coefficient between signed curl `a` and radius `|a|` under the normalized physical energy measure.  Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal D_{|a|}
=\frac{\Delta\Delta_{rad}}{E}(1-\varrho^2),
}
\]

and the squared critical tangent-gradient length is

\[
\boxed{
\|g_{|C|}\|_2^2
=\frac{\Delta_{rad}}{E}(1-\varrho^2).
}
\]

This is more informative than the amount of opposite helicity by itself.  A one-sign state has `|a|=+-a`, so `|varrho|=1` and the critical tangent volume vanishes.  An equiradial state has `Delta_rad=0`.  More generally, even with both helicity signs present, critical production is weak when sign and radius are almost perfectly locked.

For the full Euler spectral velocity, Section 14 now gives the exact geometric bound

\[
\boxed{
|K'_{NL}|
\le
2\sqrt{\frac{\Delta_{rad}}{E}}\,
\sqrt{1-\varrho^2}\,

}
\]

For one closed triad the one-dimensional tangent space makes this an equality after inserting the Waleffe speed.  Thus critical `H^{1/2}` growth is not an independent source mechanism: it is the part of the one spectral Euler velocity visible only when radial diversity and sign--radius decorrelation coexist.

## 18. The PDE itself supplies an interaction volume and a critical Reynolds barrier

The full-field obstruction identified above can be measured without choosing a packet, window, shell or coherent atom.  Let

\[
\mathcal A_{spec}:=\|F_{spec}\|_2^2
=\frac14\int\left(\frac{dS_{NL}}{d\rho}\right)^2d\rho
\]

in the discrete/absolutely-continuous sense of Section 14, and define

\[
\boxed{
V_{spec}:=\frac{\Delta}{\mathcal A_{spec}}
}
\]

when the spectral Euler current is nonzero.  Under the Navier--Stokes rescaling on `R^3`, `Delta` is critical while `A_spec` scales as inverse volume, so `V_spec` transforms exactly as a physical volume.  It is an **interaction volume inferred from the actual PDE current itself**, not a localization chosen by the analyst.

The determinant law and Cauchy give

\[
(\log\Delta)'
\le
2\beta_2\sqrt{\frac{\mathcal A_{spec}}B}
-2\nu(\lambda^2+2\beta_1^2+\alpha_1^2+\beta_2^2).

Complete the square only in the intrinsic next-curl coefficient `beta_2`:

\[
\boxed{
(\log\Delta)'
\le
\frac{\mathcal A_{spec}}{2\nu B}
-2\nu(\lambda^2+2\beta_1^2+\alpha_1^2).
}
\]

Since

\[
N^2:=\frac ZE=\lambda^2+\beta_1^2,
\]

a necessary condition for positive growth of the critical determinant is

\[
\frac{\mathcal A_{spec}}{\nu^2N^2B}>4.
\]

Define the entirely physical, scale-invariant spectral Reynolds number

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Re}_{spec}
:=\frac{\sqrt{\mathcal A_{spec}}}{\nu N\sqrt B}
=\frac1\nu\sqrt{\frac{E}{N^2V_{spec}}}.
}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\Delta' >0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Re}_{spec}>2.
}
\]

Equivalently, the PDE-defined critical interaction mass

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak M_{spec}
:=\frac{E}{N^2V_{spec}}
=\frac{\mathcal A_{spec}}{N^2B}
}
\]

must satisfy

\[
\boxed{\mathfrak M_{spec}>4\nu^2.}
\]

This recovers the familiar critical-mass dimensional combination without inserting a spatial packet.  The threshold `2` is not an event gate: it is the algebraic point at which the one Euler spectral speed can first overcome the quadratic curl killing after the exact next-Krylov coefficient has been optimized.

Thus

so any state with `Re_spec>2` is necessarily concentrating the actual velocity--defect overlap as well.  This is the precise full-PDE freedom which the isolated Waleffe bound does not control.

> **Can a Navier--Stokes trajectory keep its intrinsic spectral Reynolds number above the critical value while the PDE-defined interaction volume repeatedly collapses toward zero, despite the same curl geometry feeding quadratic viscous killing and despite isolated triad currents being self-contained martingale/phase systems?**

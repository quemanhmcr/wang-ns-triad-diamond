# Uniform service/action-or-Kelvin-flat rigidity

The coherent-cell machinery closes the reservoir/service side of the argument, but a high-transfer block need not itself have large SGS source: an exact affine/Kelvin block can transport an extremal triad with negligible curvature.  The correct dichotomy is therefore **physical service/action or gauge-quotiented flatness**, not “large transfer implies large source.”

Fix

\[
0<\tau\le1/10
\]

and define

\[
\boxed{\delta_\tau={\tau^2\over1\,036\,800\,000}.}
\]

The denominator is exactly `36*28,800,000`, coming from the weakest transition-strip H1 pair/deficit constant.

Work on the signed-good physical low-cost core, with `cond(L)<=567/500` and on the low-strain packet branch.  Exclude the already-named nonlinear sideband-feedback and large-daughter exits.  Suppose

\[
\Def_{block},\ \Def_{sideband},\ R_{pair}<\delta_\tau,
\]

\[
T J_3<\frac{\tau}{6\sqrt6},
\qquad
T J_1<\frac{\tau}{792},
\]

and the dimensionless objective strain-variation action satisfies

\[
T\int\|\dot D_{obj}\|dt<\frac{\tau}{60}.
\]

## 1. Physical Hodge flatness

For block signed-efficiency deficit below `1/20000`, the physical change-of-measure theorem gives

\[
\epsilon\ge\frac{25}{106}E_H^{phys}.
\]

The chosen `delta_tau` is much smaller than needed, hence

\[
\boxed{\sqrt{E_H^{phys}}\le\tau/3.}
\]

This measures log-scale connection curvature with actual positive child-energy transfer weights.

## 2. Objective affine-strain flatness

Let `dT` be the dimensionless non-conformal planar strain number in the co-rotating frame.  On `dT<=1/30`, either the strain stays 5%-coherent, in which case

\[
\overline{\Def}\ge(dT)^2/24,
\]

or coherence fails and

\[
T\int\|\dot D_{obj}\|dt\ge dT/20.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{dT\le\max\{\sqrt{24\epsilon},20A_{obj}\}\le\tau/3.}
\]

If the low-strain condition itself fails, that is a named high-strain/source-action branch rather than part of the flat episode.

## 3. Non-affine curvature flatness

Use the exact irreducible channel dichotomy

\[
I_B\le\sqrt6I_3+I_1.
\]

If `I_B` is non-flat, either

\[
I_3\ge I_B/(2\sqrt6)
\]

or

\[
I_1\ge I_B/2.
\]

On the H3 channel, outside source/feedback/daughter exits,

\[
\Def\ \text{or}\ R_{pair}\ge3I_3^2/4096.
\]

On the transition-strip H1 channel,

\[
\Def\ \text{or}\ R_{pair}\ge I_1^2/28\,800\,000,
\]

while dephasing requires `T J1>=I1/132`.  The chosen thresholds therefore imply

\[
\boxed{I_B\le\tau/3.}
\]

The H1 branch is the limiting constant; the H3 branch is substantially stronger.

## 4. Kelvin extremal connection

Define the gauge-quotiented flatness

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak F_K
=\sqrt{E_H^{phys}}+(dT)_{nonconf}+I_B.
}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{\mathfrak F_K\le\tau.}
\]

Common translation, rigid rotation, common affine/Kelvin transport, common-parent `SL(2)` polarization transport and material coherent-cell motion are not charged in this quantity.

Contrapositively, failure of `tau`-flatness forces a uniformly positive named branch: physical transfer/pair cost, H1/H3 source, objective-strain variation, high-strain lifetime, fresh/inherited high aspect, nonlinear daughter/feedback, or phase holonomy.  The objective-strain branch must use the **resolved transporter** source identity in `resolved_objective_strain_collision.md`; its filtered pressure Hessian routes to coherent mass or SGS service.  The high-strain branch routes to the critical normalized dissipation in `high_strain_dissipation_collision.md`, but that critical currency is not a scale-independent global reset count.

For `tau=1/100`, the clean common transfer/pair threshold is

\[
\boxed{\delta_{1/100}=1/10\,368\,000\,000\,000.}
\]

The constant is intentionally conservative.  Its purpose is to establish a genuine uniform positive gate, not to optimize the eventual master exponent.

This theorem is a precursor packet/physical-block assembly theorem.  Downstream coherent service-or-flat, moving-role, event-role and smooth first-hit theorems replace the packet-persistence part by eventwise physical roles and measurable first stops.  The flat-episode/Bellman machinery remains a supporting no-escape module; final continuum assembly, not packet synchronization, is now the programme-level frontier.

## Physical theorem

For one already event-anchored smooth carrier with the same carrier/dual through the interval, suppose:

- inserted natural/checkpoint cuts preserve the same cumulative carrier monitors and there is no named carrier first stop;
- the earlier endpoint is certified as a non-event slice of that continuing carrier;
- the physical Q^2 energy gate has `E0 >= E1/5`;
- classified residual positive physical work is strictly below its own `E1/5` owner face;
- any material membership/family changes satisfy the existing same-Q/same-psi sidecar quotient.

Then the **inherited-energy component** is persistent between-time physical carrier stock, not a newly generated positive-work charge.  It adds zero generation depth, uses no later Hahn operation, performs no FIFO/LIFO/proportional temporal deposit matching, and never moves inventory to hard interaction cells.  Material sidecars are preserved as separate ancestry/relink currency; they are not erased or declared non-events by the stock quotient.  Untyped inheritance, simultaneous residual-owner cases, role/probe changes, and genuine earlier-endpoint events fail closed to the existing routes.

The master-facing theorem projection is additive and typed.  Central-master wiring is deliberately a second certification phase; until then the legacy master keeps untyped inheritance event-facing.

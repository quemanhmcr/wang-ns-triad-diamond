# One-shot transfer-preserving Gaussian profile algebra

- random parameter checks: `100000`
- exact replacement loss at 1% profile distance: `0.030301000`
- minimum Gaussian-transfer lower bound in stress: `0.887379586`
- minimum shell-critical-L2 lower bound in stress: `2.554986052e-01`

The existence of the Gaussian approximation is supplied analytically by Christ's
near-extremizer theorem for Young convolution.  This workflow verifies only the
new deterministic transfer/mass consequences and does not pretend to numerically
certify Christ's theorem.
